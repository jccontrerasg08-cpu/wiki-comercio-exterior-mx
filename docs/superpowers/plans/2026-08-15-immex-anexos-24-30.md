# IMMEX + Anexos 24 y 30 Implementation Plan

> **Design:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-15-immex-anexos-24-30-design.md`

**Goal:** Build an authoritative, reader-first IMMEX/Anexo 24/Anexo 30 knowledge path while correcting unsupported statements in the current corpus and preserving temporal/legal governance.

**Architecture:** Official source graph -> reviewed metadata -> public explainers -> retrieval eval. The public wiki explains; canonical source/instrument registries carry provenance; corpus digests remain non-authoritative summaries. No automated status promotion.

---

## Task 4: Create the public Anexo 24 guide

**File**
- Create: `docs/wiki/programas/anexo-24-control-inventarios.md`

**Content requirements**

- Explain legal basis: Ley Aduanera art. 59-I, Decreto IMMEX art. 24-IX, RGCE 4.3.1 and Anexo 24.
- Distinguish:
  - A: minimum automated inventory control;
  - B: SECIIT for the specific rule 7.1.4 case;
  - C: inventory control for companies with Registro en el Esquema de Certificación.
- Explain minimum catalogs/modules/reports without inventing software architecture.
- Explain PEPS only where the annex mandates it.
- Explain B's <=24h electronic intake and online authority access.
- Explain C's <=48h update and online authority access.
- Add `Lo que no debe inferirse` section.
- Cite DOF/SIDOF + SAT official index.
- Add freshness note: published 2026-01-15; first published 2026 RGCE modification affects annexes 5,22,29, not 24.
