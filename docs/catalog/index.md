# Official source catalog

Mexico is the deep archive. This repository preserves primary legal documents and operational datasets when a local or release copy materially improves reproducibility, auditability, or resilience. Large originals may live in GitHub Release bundles with SHA-256 checksums, while document-level manifests keep the official URL, hash and byte size.

The official publication remains the legal authority. Archive presence, a successful download, or HTTP 200 never decides legal currentness.

Structured TIGIE/NICO tables live in [arancel-mx](https://github.com/jccontrerasg08-cpu/arancel-mx); this wiki keeps the legal/documentary provenance layer rather than a competing tariff database. Proprietary sources such as Incoterms® remain reference-only: do not vendor ICC rule text.
